# Source ledger

Every source used to build this corpus, with what kind of source it is, its date or version, and **why it was judged acceptable** under cite-or-refuse rules. 526 rows across thirteen research angles.

This file is the restored evidence-provenance layer. The thirteen raw research files it came from were deleted from the skill on 2026-07-28; their ledgers are reproduced here **verbatim**, grouped by the angle that compiled them. Nothing has been merged or deduplicated: a source cited by two angles appears twice, because the "why acceptable" judgement was made in that angle's context and the two judgements are not always identical. That duplication is information — a source two independent researchers both reached is better attested than one only one of them found.

**What is not here.** The raw files also carried *Current state of knowledge*, *Superseded*, *Unresolved* and *Verification log* sections. Those were folded into `dossier.md` (§§1–8, §9, §11) and `handbook.md` (§6, §7). The full raw files remain recoverable from the `me` repository at commit `15e599a`, under `reference/health/tcc/raw/` — for example `git show 15e599a:reference/health/tcc/raw/guidelines.md`.

**How to use it.** Two questions it answers that `dossier.md` cannot: *is this class of source admissible here* (read the "Why acceptable" column of comparable rows), and *was this particular claim read at source or only via an abstract* — many rows say so explicitly, and a row that says "abstract only" or "read via a secondary rendering" is exactly the kind of claim that carries a `?` in the dossier.

Column shapes differ slightly between angles; they are reproduced as compiled rather than normalised.
